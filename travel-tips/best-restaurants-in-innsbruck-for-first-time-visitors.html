The best restaurants in Innsbruck for first-time visitors

Innsbruck's food makes most sense with the mountains in view: dumplings, cheese noodles and pan-fried potatoes, the cooking of Tyrol. Mix old-town restaurants with a meal at a mountain hut.

Where to eat

The Altstadt

The old town has traditional inns and restaurants serving Tyrolean cooking in historic rooms. Convenient and atmospheric.

Around Maria-Theresien-Straße

Innsbruck's main street and the streets around it have cafés, restaurants and more international choices.

Wilten

South of the centre, the Wilten district has neighbourhood restaurants and inns with a more local feel.

The Nordkette and mountain huts

The Nordkette cable car rises from the city to the mountains, and huts along the trails serve hearty food with views. A mountain lunch is one of the best meals in Innsbruck.

A first-timer's shortlist

DishWhat it is
Tiroler GröstlPan-fried potato, onion and meat topped with a fried egg
KäsespätzleSoft egg noodles layered with cheese and fried onions
SpeckknödelBread dumplings with smoked bacon, in broth or with sauerkraut
SchlutzkrapfenTyrolean filled pasta, often with spinach and cheese
KaiserschmarrnShredded caramelised pancake with fruit compote
KiachlFried dough, sweet or savoury
Good to knowKaiserschmarrn is filling enough to be a meal on its own. Share one after a hike.

Plan by the day

Hearty dishes like Gröstl and Käsespätzle suit a lunch after a morning on the mountain. Keep dinner lighter, or save Kaiserschmarrn for a late afternoon treat.

Mountain huts

Huts keep seasonal hours, and some close between seasons. Check before you set off, and carry offline maps: signal can drop on mountain trails.

Common questions

What is Tiroler Gröstl?

Pan-fried potato, onion and meat topped with a fried egg, a Tyrolean classic.

Can I eat at a mountain hut from Innsbruck?

Yes. The Nordkette cable car and trails lead to huts that serve food, though hours are seasonal.

What is Kaiserschmarrn?

A shredded, caramelised pancake served with fruit compote.
