The best local food areas in Ho Chi Minh City

Ho Chi Minh City is built for eating by district. Rice plates, noodles, bánh mì and shellfish all taste more like the real thing once you leave District 1 for at least one meal.

Four areas to eat in

District 1

The centre, where most visitors stay, has everything from street stalls to smart restaurants. Convenient, but often pricier and more tourist-facing.

District 3

Next door, District 3 has many local favourites, including busy rice and noodle shops, at everyday prices.

District 5 and Chợ Lớn

The city's Chinatown, around Chợ Lớn, is known for Chinese-Vietnamese cooking: noodle soups, roast meats and dim sum-style dishes.

Phú Nhuận and Bình Thạnh

Residential districts north of the centre, with neighbourhood restaurants and evening street food where locals eat.

What to order

DishWhat it is
Cơm tấmBroken rice with grilled pork, egg and pickles, one of the city's defining everyday meals
Bánh mìSaigon-style baguette with pâté, cold cuts, pork, herbs and pickles
Hủ tiếuSouthern noodle soup, served dry or in broth, often with pork and seafood
Bánh xèoCrisp turmeric pancake with pork, shrimp and bean sprouts, wrapped in herbs
ỐcSnails and shellfish cooked with lemongrass, coconut, butter or chilli
Bò lá lốtSeasoned beef wrapped in betel leaf and grilled
Good to knowCơm tấm is a breakfast and lunch favourite. Many of the best places are busiest in the morning.

Meal timing

Saigon eats all day. Plan cơm tấm or bánh mì in the morning, noodle soup at lunch, and ốc or bánh xèo in the evening.

Getting between districts

Grab is the easiest way to move between districts, by car or motorbike. Carry small cash for street stalls, and keep translation ready for menus without English.

Common questions

What is cơm tấm?

Broken rice with grilled pork, egg and pickles, one of Ho Chi Minh City's defining everyday meals.

Where is Ho Chi Minh City's Chinatown?

In District 5, around Chợ Lớn.

Is District 1 the best place to eat?

It is convenient, but District 3 and other neighbourhoods often have more local food at lower prices.
