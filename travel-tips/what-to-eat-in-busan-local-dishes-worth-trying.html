What to eat in Busan: local dishes worth trying

Busan is a port city, and its food shows it: raw fish by the market, fish cakes everywhere, and a milky pork soup that locals swear by. Match each dish to the district you are exploring.

Six dishes to look for

Dwaeji gukbap

Milky pork-bone broth with rice and sliced pork, one of Busan's signature comfort foods. Season it yourself with the condiments on the table.

Milmyeon

Chilled wheat noodles developed in Busan, especially refreshing in summer.

Hoe

Korean-style raw fish, closely tied to Busan's port identity. At Jagalchi Market, you can choose fish downstairs and have it prepared upstairs.

Eomuk

Busan fish cakes, sold on skewers in broth or in many shapes from specialist shops. A quick snack or light meal.

Ssiat hotteok

A sweet pancake stuffed with seeds and nuts, a Busan variation on the classic hotteok.

Grilled eel

A richer option, often served with spicy sauce and shared side dishes.

Good to knowBusan's fish cakes are famous across Korea. Specialist shops sell them as gifts, too.
