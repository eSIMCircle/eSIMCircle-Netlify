The best local food areas in Lucerne

Lucerne is expensive, even by Swiss standards. The smart approach is to spend selectively: one classic Swiss meal, one simple lunch and one dinner where the location is worth the price.

Four areas to eat in

The Old Town

The squares and lanes of the Altstadt have traditional restaurants serving Swiss and Lucerne classics. This is the place for your one big Swiss meal.

The Reuss river and lakefront

Restaurants along the river and the lake charge for the view. Choose one for an evening when the setting matters.

Neustadt and Bruch

The newer districts south and west of the station have more everyday restaurants, cafés and international food at slightly lower prices.

Mountain excursions

On trips up Pilatus, Rigi or other mountains, restaurants at the top or along the way make lunch part of the day out.

Spending wisely

Make lunch the bigger meal; set lunch menus are often better value than dinner. Use bakeries and supermarket takeaway for one meal a day if you are watching costs.

Getting around

The Old Town and lakefront are walkable. Lake boats and trains reach the mountains. Keep your map and tickets handy; mountain areas can have weaker signal.

Common questions

What is Luzerner Chügelipastete?

Lucerne's signature dish: a puff-pastry shell filled with creamy veal and mushroom ragout.

How can I eat cheaply in Lucerne?

Make lunch the main meal with a set menu, and use bakeries or supermarket takeaway for another.

Is fondue a lunch or dinner dish?

Either, though it is heavy, so many people choose it for a relaxed dinner.
