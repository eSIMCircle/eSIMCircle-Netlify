The best restaurants in Beijing for first-time visitors

Beijing's food mixes imperial dishes, northern wheat staples and neighbourhood specialities. A first visit should make room for duck, noodles and hotpot, rather than treating every meal as a banquet.

Where to eat

Qianmen and Dashilar

South of Tiananmen Square, these restored streets have long-established restaurants and snack shops, including some of the city's best-known duck restaurants.

Dongcheng and Wangfujing

The central Dongcheng district, around Wangfujing and the hutongs, has restaurants of every level, from old-style noodle houses to formal dining.

Sanlitun and Chaoyang

East of the centre, Sanlitun is Beijing's international district, with modern Chinese restaurants alongside foreign cuisines.

Niujie

In the south-west, the Niujie area around its historic mosque is known for Hui Muslim food, including lamb dishes and halal snacks.

Good to knowPeking duck is a sit-down occasion. Popular duck restaurants are busy, so book ahead and order a whole or half duck to share.

Don't banquet every meal

Beijing's everyday food is as good as its formal dining. Mix one duck dinner with noodle lunches and a hotpot evening.

Paying and ordering

Alipay and WeChat Pay are standard, and many restaurants take orders by QR code at the table. Set up a payment app before the trip, carry a backup, and keep translation ready.
