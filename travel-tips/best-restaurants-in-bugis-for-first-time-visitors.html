The best restaurants in Bugis for first-time visitors

Bugis is one of the best neighbourhoods in Singapore for a first food day. Malay, Indian-Muslim, Chinese and modern dining sit within a short walk of one another.

eSIMCircle Editorial · Published July 31, 2026 · Updated July 31, 2026 · 3 min read

Where to eat

Kampong Glam and Arab Street

Around Sultan Mosque, Kampong Glam has long-running Malay and Indian-Muslim restaurants, famous for murtabak, alongside Middle Eastern cafés and modern places on Haji Lane.

Bugis Junction and Bugis+

The malls next to the MRT have food courts and chain restaurants, useful for a quick, air-conditioned meal.

Beach Road and North Bridge Road

These long roads have a mix of local restaurants and coffee shops, including some long-established names.

Rochor and Albert Centre

Albert Centre is a hawker centre with many local dishes at local prices. It is the place for a proper hawker meal.

Good to knowMurtabak and nasi padang are lunchtime favourites in Kampong Glam. Some stalls sell out by late afternoon.

Mix it up

Use hawker centres for most meals; they are cheap, fast and excellent. Save one dinner for a sit-down restaurant in Kampong Glam or along Beach Road.

Hawker stalls may take cash or local QR payments; card acceptance varies. Restaurants usually take cards.

Common questions

What is murtabak?

Griddled bread stuffed with egg, meat and spices, a speciality of Kampong Glam.

Where is the best hawker centre near Bugis?

Albert Centre, a short walk from Bugis MRT.

Do Bugis restaurants take cards?

Most restaurants do. Hawker stalls vary, so carry some cash.
