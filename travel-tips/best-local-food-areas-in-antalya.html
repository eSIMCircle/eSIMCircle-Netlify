The best local food areas in Antalya

Antalya's food is Mediterranean Turkish at its most relaxed: grilled meat, fresh fish, and a local white-bean salad dressed with tahini that you will not find made quite the same way elsewhere in Turkey.

Four areas to eat in

Kaleiçi

The old town is where most visitors eat, with restaurants in courtyards and on terraces above the harbour. The atmosphere is the draw. Prices on the most tourist-facing streets can be high, so compare a few menus before sitting down.

Lara

East of the centre, Lara is a long stretch of hotels and beach clubs, with restaurants serving visitors staying along the coast.

Konyaaltı

West of the centre, the Konyaaltı beach promenade has relaxed places for an evening meal near the sea.

Muratpaşa

The central district around the old town is where many locals eat. Step away from Kaleiçi into the surrounding streets for everyday kebab houses and lokantas at local prices.

Good to knowPiyaz and şiş köfte are a lunchtime classic in Antalya. Look for a place that specialises in them rather than ordering from a long tourist menu.

Planning your meals

Some signature dishes make more sense at breakfast or lunch than late at night. Plan a piyaz lunch, keep dinner for grilled fish by the sea, and leave one meal open for a place you find while walking.

Getting around

Antalya's tram and taxis connect the old town with Lara and Konyaaltı. A working map helps you find the small places just outside Kaleiçi, and translation apps help with menus that are only in Turkish.

Common questions

What is Antalya piyaz?

A white bean salad dressed with tahini, garlic and vinegar, richer than versions elsewhere in Turkey.

Is Kaleiçi expensive for food?

It can be on the most tourist-facing streets. The surrounding Muratpaşa streets are often better value.

What should I eat for dessert in Antalya?

Kabak tatlısı, candied pumpkin, often served with tahini or nuts.
