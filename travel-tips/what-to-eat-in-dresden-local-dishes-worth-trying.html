What to eat in Dresden: local dishes worth trying

Dresden pairs hearty Saxon cooking with a lively modern food scene. Eat the classics in the old town, then cross the river to the Neustadt for a wider menu.

eSIMCircle Editorial · Published May 28, 2025 · Updated June 27, 2026 · 3 min read

Six dishes to look for

Sächsischer Sauerbraten

Marinated roast meat with a sweet-sour sauce, often served with dumplings and red cabbage. A traditional Sunday dish.

Quarkkeulchen

Small fried cakes of quark and potato, usually served sweet with apple sauce or cinnamon sugar.

Dresdner Eierschecke

A layered cake of pastry, quark and custard, Dresden's own. Order it with coffee in the afternoon.

Kartoffelsuppe

Potato soup, a simple Saxon comfort dish, especially in cold weather.

Bratwurst

Regional sausages, the easy casual option at stands and markets.

Pfefferkuchen

Spiced gingerbread from Saxony, especially in the colder months.

Good to knowAt Christmas, Dresden's Stollen, a rich fruit bread, is everywhere. The city's Striezelmarkt is named after it.

Getting around

Trams link the Altstadt, Neustadt and the riverside districts. Cards are widely accepted, but carry some cash for market stalls.
