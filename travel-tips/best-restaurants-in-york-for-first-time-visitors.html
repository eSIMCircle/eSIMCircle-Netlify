The best restaurants in York for first-time visitors

York's compact centre makes restaurant planning easy. You can walk between medieval streets, the river and several good dining pockets without losing sightseeing time.

Where to eat

Fossgate and Walmgate

A short street lined with independent restaurants and cafés, one of York's best areas for dinner.

Stonegate and the city centre

Around Stonegate, near the Minster, historic pubs and restaurants sit in old timber-framed buildings.

Micklegate

Running from the station side into the centre, Micklegate has pubs, restaurants and bars.

Bishopthorpe Road

South of the centre, "Bishy Road" is a local high street with independent food shops and cafés.

Good to knowSunday roast is a British institution. Pubs serving good roasts get busy, so book ahead for Sunday lunch.

Pubs and restaurants

Many of York's historic pubs serve food, and some are as good as restaurants. They are also warmer and cheaper on a cold evening.

Booking

Popular restaurants fill up at weekends and in summer. Book the dinner you most want and leave the rest flexible.

Common questions

Where is the best area for dinner in York?

Fossgate and Walmgate have a strong run of independent restaurants.

What is parkin?

A sticky ginger and oat cake associated with Yorkshire.

Do I need to book a Sunday roast in York?

For popular pubs, yes. They get busy on Sundays.
