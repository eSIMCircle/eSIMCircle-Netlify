Where to eat in Sydney without wasting your trip

The best food plan for Sydney mixes harbour-side seafood with the city's multicultural neighbourhoods. You do not need to pay for a view at every meal.

Four areas to eat in

The CBD and Circular Quay

Harbour-side restaurants with famous views, and prices to match. Choose one meal here for the setting.

Surry Hills

South of the centre, Surry Hills is one of Sydney's best eating neighbourhoods, with cafés, wine bars and modern restaurants.

Chippendale and Haymarket

Near Central Station, Haymarket and Chinatown have excellent Chinese, Thai, Vietnamese, Korean and Japanese food, while Chippendale adds modern restaurants.

Newtown and Enmore

The inner west's bohemian high streets are full of affordable restaurants from around the world.

Spending wisely

Pay for the harbour view once. Spend the other meals in Surry Hills, Haymarket or Newtown, where the food is often better value and more interesting.

Tipping is not expected in Australia, though rounding up for good service is appreciated.

Getting around

Trains, light rail and buses connect the main eating areas, and contactless payment works across the network.
