Where to eat in Lyon without wasting your trip

Lyon is called France's food capital, and it is easy to overdo it. Mix one traditional bouchon meal with lighter market and neighbourhood dining, so you are not facing three heavy meals in a row.

What a bouchon is

A bouchon is a traditional Lyon restaurant serving hearty local dishes in a convivial, often crowded room. Not every place calling itself a bouchon is traditional, so look at the menu: real bouchons serve the dishes below.

The dishes to know

DishWhat it is
Quenelle de brochetA soft pike dumpling, usually in a rich sauce
Salade lyonnaiseFrisée, lardons, croutons and a poached egg
Saucisson briochéSausage baked in buttery brioche
Tablier de sapeurBreaded tripe, for adventurous eaters
Cervelle de canutFresh cheese with herbs and shallot, despite its name ("silk worker's brain")
Praline tartA bright pink praline tart, Lyon's signature sweet
Good to knowSalade lyonnaise makes a good light meal on a day when you have a bouchon dinner planned.

What meals cost

Before publication, a quick casual meal was about €12 to €20, a comfortable sit-down meal €25 to €45, and a premium meal €70 or more. Set lunch menus (formules) are often the best value.

Booking

Popular bouchons fill up, especially at weekends. Book dinner ahead.

Common questions

What is a bouchon in Lyon?

A traditional Lyon restaurant serving hearty local dishes in a convivial setting.

What is a quenelle?

A soft dumpling, classically made with pike, usually served in a rich sauce.

Where is the Halles Paul Bocuse?

Near Part-Dieu. It is Lyon's covered food market.
