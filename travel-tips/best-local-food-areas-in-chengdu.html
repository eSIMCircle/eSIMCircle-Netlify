The best local food areas in Chengdu

Chengdu is a city of bold Sichuan flavours, but not every meal needs maximum heat. Hotpot, noodles, cold dishes and tea-house snacks each show a different side of how the city eats.

eSIMCircle Editorial · Published October 7, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read

Four areas to eat in

Chunxi Road and Taikoo Li

The central shopping district has restaurants of every kind, from chains to well-known hotpot places, and is easy to reach by metro.

Kuanzhai Alleys

The restored lanes mix restaurants, tea houses and snack counters. Busy with visitors, but convenient.

Yulin

A neighbourhood with a more local feel, known for small restaurants and evening food.

Wuhou and Jinli

Around the Wuhou Shrine and the Jinli lanes, snack streets sit next to restaurants serving Sichuan classics.

What to order

DishWhat it is
Mapo tofuSilken tofu with minced meat, chilli and Sichuan pepper, the classic hot-and-numbing balance
Dan dan noodlesSmall noodles with chilli, sesame or preserved vegetables, an easy first lunch
Sichuan hotpotA communal pot of chilli, beef tallow and Sichuan pepper
ChuànchuànSkewers cooked in spicy broth, more casual than full hotpot
Fuqi feipianChilled sliced beef and offal in chilli and pepper dressing
Sweet-water noodlesThick noodles with sweet soy, chilli and sesame
Good to knowMany hotpot restaurants offer a split pot, with spicy broth on one side and mild on the other. It is a good way to try it without overdoing the heat.

Managing the spice

Sichuan cooking uses chilli heat and the numbing tingle of Sichuan pepper. Ask for wēi là (微辣), slightly spicy, if you are unsure. Balance a fiery meal with a sweeter or milder one the next time.

Paying and ordering

Alipay and WeChat Pay are standard. Many restaurants order by QR code at the table. Set up a payment app before the trip, and keep translation apps ready for menus.

Common questions

What should I eat first in Chengdu?

Mapo tofu or dan dan noodles for lunch, and Sichuan hotpot for a group dinner.

Can I get hotpot that is not too spicy?

Yes. Many places offer a split pot with spicy and mild broths.

How do I pay at restaurants in Chengdu?

Alipay and WeChat Pay are standard. Set one up before you travel.
