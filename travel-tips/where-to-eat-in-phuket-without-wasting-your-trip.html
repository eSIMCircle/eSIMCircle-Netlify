Where to eat in Phuket without wasting your trip

Phuket's food is more than beach seafood. The island's Chinese-Thai trading history lives on in Phuket Old Town's dishes, while the coast is best for fresh fish and shellfish.

Four areas to eat in

Phuket Old Town

The island's historic town, with colourful Sino-Portuguese shophouses, is where to find Phuket's own dishes. Many locals eat here.

Rawai

At the island's southern tip, Rawai's seafood market lets you buy fresh seafood and have it cooked at nearby restaurants.

Kata and Karon

Beach areas with a mix of Thai and international restaurants, popular with families.

Patong

The busiest beach town, with every kind of food. Convenient but often pricier.

Good to knowKhanom jeen is a breakfast favourite in Phuket. Look for shops with a spread of fresh herbs and vegetables on the table.

Plan your day around the Old Town

Spend a morning in Phuket Old Town for breakfast and lunch, then head back to the coast for a seafood dinner.

Practical notes

Phuket is larger than many visitors expect, and traffic can be slow. Grab and Bolt are widely used. At seafood markets, ask the price by weight and the cooking charge before ordering.

Common questions

What is moo hong?

Slow-braised pork belly with pepper and soy, a Phuket Old Town speciality.

Where can I buy seafood and have it cooked in Phuket?

At the Rawai seafood market, where nearby restaurants cook what you buy.

Is Patong good for food?

It has a lot of choice, but Phuket Old Town is better for local dishes.
