The best restaurants in Yogyakarta for first-time visitors

Yogyakarta's food is deeply Javanese: sweet-savoury gudeg, charcoal-grilled goat satay and noodles cooked over coals. The best meals are often in simple warungs rather than smart restaurants.

Where to eat

Malioboro and central Yogyakarta

The city centre has gudeg restaurants, warungs and evening street food. Convenient for a first meal.

Prawirotaman

South of the palace, Prawirotaman is popular with travelers, with cafés and restaurants serving Indonesian and international food.

Kotagede

The old silver-working district south-east of the centre has traditional food and snacks in a historic setting.

Bantul and the southern outskirts

South of the city, Bantul is known for sate klathak, goat satay grilled on bicycle-spoke skewers. Worth the trip.

Good to knowGudeg is sweet, which surprises many visitors. It is traditionally eaten for breakfast or lunch.

Warung meals

A warung is a small, simple eating place, often family run. Many of Yogyakarta's best dishes are served this way. Point at what you want if there is no menu, and pay at the end.

Getting around

Grab and Gojek make it easy to reach Bantul or Kotagede. Carry cash for warungs, and keep translation ready.

Common questions

What is gudeg?

Young jackfruit slow-cooked with coconut milk and palm sugar, Yogyakarta's signature dish.

Where can I eat sate klathak?

In Bantul, south of the city, where it is a local speciality.

What is a warung?

A small, simple eating place, often family run, serving local dishes.
