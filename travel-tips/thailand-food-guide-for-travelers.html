A Thailand food guide for travelers, focused on Pattaya

Pattaya's advantage is easy access to Gulf seafood and a huge range of Thai restaurants. Use the beach districts for convenience, but do not assume the waterfront is always the best value.

Four areas to eat in

Naklua

North of central Pattaya, Naklua is known for seafood restaurants, many popular with Thai visitors. A good place for a seafood dinner.

Central Pattaya

The busiest area, with every kind of restaurant from street stalls to international chains. Convenient but crowded.

Jomtien

South of the centre, Jomtien's beachfront and side streets have seafood places and a night market.

Pratumnak

The quieter hill between Pattaya and Jomtien has smaller restaurants and a more relaxed feel.

Good to knowAt seafood restaurants, prices are often by weight. Ask the price per kilo or per 100 grams before your choice goes into the kitchen.

Spice levels

Thai food can be very spicy. Ask for "mai phet" (not spicy) or "phet nit noi" (a little spicy) if you are unsure.

Beyond Pattaya

Thai regional food varies: northern curries, north-eastern salads and grilled meats, southern seafood and spice. Wherever you go, a busy local restaurant is a better bet than a long tourist menu.

Common questions

Where is the best seafood in Pattaya?

Naklua, north of central Pattaya, is known for seafood restaurants.

How do I order less spicy food in Thailand?

Ask for "mai phet" (not spicy) or "phet nit noi" (a little spicy).

How is seafood priced in Pattaya?

Often by weight. Ask the price before ordering.
