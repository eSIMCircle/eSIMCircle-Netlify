Where to eat in Gangnam without wasting your trip

Gangnam is polished and spread out. Barbecue, soups and modern Korean restaurants are easiest to enjoy when you choose the subdistrict first and the restaurant second.

What to order

Korean barbecue

Beef or pork grilled at your table, with side dishes. It is one of the most dependable group meals in Gangnam. Most barbecue restaurants expect a minimum of two people.

Samgyeopsal

Thick slices of pork belly grilled and wrapped in lettuce with garlic, sauces and kimchi.

Naengmyeon

Cold buckwheat noodles in icy broth or spicy sauce. A refreshing finish after grilled meat.

Seolleongtang

Milky ox-bone soup, served plain so you season it yourself with salt and spring onion. Good for a cheap, warming lunch.

Gopchang

Grilled intestines, a popular late-night meal for adventurous eaters.

Korean fried chicken

Crisp chicken, plain or sauced, with beer. A favourite for casual evenings.

Good to knowMany barbecue restaurants have staff who grill for you. If not, watch the table next to you, or ask.

Practical notes

Gangnam's station exits are far apart; save the exit with each restaurant. Use Naver Map or KakaoMap, and a translation app for menus. Cards are accepted almost everywhere.

Common questions

Can I eat Korean barbecue alone?

Many barbecue restaurants expect at least two people. Some solo-friendly places exist; check first.

What is seolleongtang?

A milky ox-bone soup served plain, which you season yourself.

Where is the best area in Gangnam for a casual dinner?

Around Gangnam Station, which is busy and open late.
