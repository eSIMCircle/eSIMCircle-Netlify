The best local food areas in Valencia

Valencia's food rule is simple: paella is a lunch dish. Build the day around rice at midday and keep the evening for seafood, tapas and lighter local plates.

eSIMCircle Editorial · Published January 18, 2026 · Updated March 30, 2026 · 3 min read

Four areas to eat in

Ruzafa

One of Valencia's liveliest neighbourhoods, with modern restaurants, tapas bars and international food. Good for dinner.

El Cabanyal

The old fishing quarter by the beach, home to restaurants serving rice dishes and seafood. The natural place for a long paella lunch.

Ciutat Vella and El Carmen

The old town and the El Carmen district have traditional bars and restaurants close to the main sights.

The Albufera

South of the city, the lagoon and rice fields of the Albufera are where paella's ingredients come from. Villages such as El Palmar have restaurants specialising in rice and in all i pebre.

Common questions

Does real paella valenciana contain seafood?

No. The original style uses chicken, rabbit and beans. Seafood rice dishes include arroz a banda.

When should I eat paella in Valencia?

At lunch. Paella is traditionally a midday dish.

Where can I eat paella near the rice fields?

In the Albufera, south of the city, in villages such as El Palmar.
