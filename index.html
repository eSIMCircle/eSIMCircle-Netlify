Our food guides cover the areas worth eating in, the dishes to order first and what a meal costs. Our eSIM guides cover the data side, so maps, menus and reservations work from the first meal.

Antalya, Turkey

The best local food areas in Antalya

Antalya's food is Mediterranean Turkish at its most relaxed: grilled meat, fresh fish, and a local white-bean salad dressed with tahini that you will not find made quite the same way elsewhere in Turkey.

Bologna, Italy

The best local food areas in Bologna

Bologna earned its nickname La Grassa, the fat one, through fresh egg pasta, cured meat and slow sauces. The best first trip spreads those dishes across the day rather than ordering everything at one sitting.

Chengdu, China

The best local food areas in Chengdu

Chengdu is a city of bold Sichuan flavours, but not every meal needs maximum heat. Hotpot, noodles, cold dishes and tea-house snacks each show a different side of how the city eats.
