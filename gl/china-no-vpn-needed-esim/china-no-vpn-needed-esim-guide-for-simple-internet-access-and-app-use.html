Apps that make travel data essential

For everyday apps and messaging, most data use comes from small actions repeated all day: maps, messages, translation, restaurant searches, ticket confirmations, and ride apps. Hotel Wi-Fi should handle heavy app updates, video, and cloud backups. If you will share hotspot data with a laptop or another traveler, plan for a higher daily allowance. If you only need maps and messaging between Wi-Fi stops, a smaller fixed-data plan may be enough.
