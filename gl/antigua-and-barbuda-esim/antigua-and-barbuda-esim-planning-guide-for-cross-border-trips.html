The comparison criteria that matter most

For Antigua and Barbuda, this topic is about cross-border travel. Treat that as the planning lens for the whole page. Check how many days you need, which apps you will rely on away from Wi-Fi, whether hotspot matters, and when you want to install the eSIM. A good plan should make arrival, transfers, and everyday navigation feel uneventful.

Data habits that change the right plan choice

For cross-border travel, most data use comes from small actions repeated all day: maps, messages, translation, restaurant searches, ticket confirmations, and ride apps. Hotel Wi-Fi should handle heavy app updates, video, and cloud backups. If you will share hotspot data with a laptop or another traveler, plan for a higher daily allowance. If you only need maps and messaging between Wi-Fi stops, a smaller fixed-data plan may be enough.
