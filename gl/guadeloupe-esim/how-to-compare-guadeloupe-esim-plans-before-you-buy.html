A simple framework for choosing the right option

For Guadeloupe, this topic is about comparing plans before you buy. Treat that as the planning lens for the whole page. Check how many days you need, which apps you will rely on away from Wi-Fi, whether hotspot matters, and when you want to install the eSIM. A good plan should make arrival, transfers, and everyday navigation feel uneventful.

  • Use daily plans for uncertain usage patterns.
  • Compare total data, validity, and route complexity together.
  • Check whether your phone supports eSIM before comparing prices.
  • Use fixed-data plans when your usage is predictable.
