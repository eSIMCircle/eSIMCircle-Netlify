Data habits that change the right plan choice

For avoiding roaming fees, most data use comes from small actions repeated all day: maps, messages, translation, restaurant searches, ticket confirmations, and ride apps. Hotel Wi-Fi should handle heavy app updates, video, and cloud backups. If you will share hotspot data with a laptop or another traveler, plan for a higher daily allowance. If you only need maps and messaging between Wi-Fi stops, a smaller fixed-data plan may be enough.

Common questions

Should I just buy the cheapest Jersey plan?

If it covers every day of your trip and your busiest day, yes. If it does not, a slightly bigger plan saves you buying twice.

Which plan is best for a long stay?

For the longest validity, look at Jersey eSIM 20GB 30 Days: 20GB over 30 days for 16.55 USD.

Can I keep my regular SIM on?

Yes. Keep it for calls and texts and use the eSIM for data.
